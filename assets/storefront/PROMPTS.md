# Storefront image prompts

All eight assets were generated with Codex's built-in image-generation tool, then converted to optimized WebP for the local WooCommerce storefront. No logos or product photos from other sites were used. The images are original, concept-only product photography.

## Demo mug — `demo-mug.webp`

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: WooCommerce product listing and product detail image, square crop. Primary request: premium studio product photograph of a modern ceramic coffee mug for a small AI-commerce demo store. Subject: single substantial ivory ceramic mug, subtle deep-indigo geometric diamond motif on its front (no letters, no brand wordmark), realistic curved handle, faint steam, tactile matte glaze. Scene/backdrop: pale warm-gray and lavender seamless studio surface with a soft lavender cast shadow; clean, sophisticated, editorial retail aesthetic. Composition/framing: mug centered and fully visible with generous margin for square catalog crop, three-quarter angle at eye level. Lighting/mood: refined softbox lighting, crisp material texture, natural shadow. Color palette: ivory, deep ink navy, restrained violet accents. Constraints: one mug only, no people, no text, no watermark, no extra objects, no mock UI, realistic and polished.

## Store hero and page background — `store-hero.webp`

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: full-width website hero background and subtle page-wide visual texture for a WooCommerce demo store connected to an AI gateway. Primary request: sophisticated editorial studio scene for a modern technology-and-commerce showcase, showing an ivory ceramic coffee mug with a tiny indigo diamond emblem and a deep navy heavyweight hoodie with a tiny violet diamond embroidery, coordinated with separate catalog product shots. Scene/backdrop: warm ivory architectural studio with softly curved walls and layered stone plinths, faint translucent violet light ribbons crossing the background like a tasteful suggestion of connected services, no visible computer screens. Composition/framing: very wide landscape crop; product display predominantly on the RIGHT half, LEFT half deliberately calm and low-detail for dark overlay headline text; plenty of open space, horizon low. Lighting/mood: natural directional window light with soft violet bounce, premium and confident, photographic realism. Color palette: warm ivory, ink navy, electric-violet accents, quiet slate. Constraints: no typography, no letters, no logos, no UI elements, no watermark; merchandise should be clear but not oversized; suitable for cover background with readable text overlay.
